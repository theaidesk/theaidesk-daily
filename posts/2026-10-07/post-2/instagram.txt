Most countries rent their AI from a handful of US labs. South Korea wants to own one.

The country's Ministry of Science and ICT plans to spend 4.7 trillion won, about US$3.5 billion, on a state-led frontier AI model. Work is due to start in March 2027.

• The government would put in direct equity, with private money alongside
• More than 80% of the budget, 3.9 trillion won, is earmarked for 10,000 Nvidia GPUs
• A lead developer is chosen by competitive tender, open to single companies, consortiums or special-purpose vehicles
• The stated aim is less dependence on foreign AI models

The honest part: this is a plan, not a spent budget. Parliament still has to approve the 2027 budget in December, and no developer has been picked yet.

What it means for you: if you live outside the US or China, expect more governments to fund their own models, and to spend most of it on chips.

Source: Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT, October 5 2026.
