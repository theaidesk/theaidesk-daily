Waiting for ChatGPT to draw your image? That wait is about to become ad space.

OpenAI announced a new visual ad format that will start testing later this month in the US, with an initial group of advertisers. The ads appear while an image is being generated.

• Ads are clearly labeled and stay separate from the image you asked for
• Images in the ads can show a product, how it's used, or what a service makes possible
• Advertisers also get new tools to track purchases, plus brand-safety checks meant to keep ads out of sensitive conversations

The honest part: OpenAI says ads don't influence ChatGPT's answers. That's the company's own statement, and nobody outside has checked it. It's also a test, not a full rollout.

What it means for you: if you use ChatGPT's image tool in the US, you may start seeing sponsored images in the wait. Nothing changes for you today.
