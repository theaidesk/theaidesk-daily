Three people who worked on OpenAI's safety team are out, and the company and the reporting tell it a little differently.

What OpenAI says: it "parted ways with three individuals" after an investigation found they mishandled sensitive information outside established company procedures.

What the Wall Street Journal reports: the three shared confidential details with a third-party AI-safety organization.

What OpenAI also says: its probe found no evidence of a compromise or a vulnerability.

What we can't tell you: what exactly was shared, or how the researchers see it. All of this is the company's own account of its own staffing decision, and no independent check has been published.

Why it matters for non-experts: safety teams are the people who test whether these systems behave. How a company handles disagreement with them is a signal worth tracking. Nothing for you to change today.

Source: OpenAI spokesperson statement via the Wall Street Journal, Oct 1
