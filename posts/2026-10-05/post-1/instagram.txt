What happens when a city council asks the biggest AI companies to swear to their answers?

New York City Council held a rare hearing today with all 51 members, and Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and Meta were set to testify under oath about the risks of AI.

• Council wants the labs to explain how they will keep AI agents from going out of control
• Each company is sending a senior policy or safety person
• Council has also floated its own proposals on AI protections for New Yorkers

The honest part: this is a hearing, not a law. The reporting we found was written before testimony started, so we can't tell you what was said or whether anything changes.

What it means for you: cities and states are not waiting for Washington. What these companies say under oath can end up shaping rules that apply to the tools you use.

Source: NYC Council, CNBC, October 5 2026.
